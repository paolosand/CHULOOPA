Looping systems are inherently repetitive — the same pattern cycles indefinitely, limiting the musical dynamics of a live performance. CHULOOPA is a dynamic drum looper that uses beatbox input to create patterns, then autonomously varies them based on the performer's live musical energy, or "spice" — a dBFS-mapped RMS measure of the live audio environment. At loop boundaries, one of 5 pre-generated variations (from rhythmic creator, a GPT-style causal Transformer decoder) is selected to match the current spice level. These five variants are generated in parallel at varying temperatures, then sorted by deviation score to maintain a reliable creativity spectrum. Variations span a spectrum from subtle to adventurous (spice 0.2–1.0). CHULOOPA demonstrates that inherent repetition need not be a constraint: with AI mediation, the loop becomes dynamically expressive without losing its musical identity.
